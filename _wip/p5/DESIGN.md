## New renderer pieces (built before the places, so authors only write data)
- GROUNDS (16 new): bath kitchen brick lawn rubber seabed pool dock carpet desert dig mine forest temple pinball moon felt grid choc — reused: tarmac snow sand grass cloud.
- BLOCK_LOOKS (8 new): counter crates coral logs (raised); quicksand tar crater choc (flat).
- flow looks (3 new): current ski choc. spin look (1 new): whirl. portal looks (2 new): lift tunnel.
- dead looks brought to life: bridge `stone` (temple), track `wire` (mountain), spins turntable/record/pizza.

## Not built (and why)
- A ghost / spider / haunted place: 👻 and 🕸️ are on the NOTHING-ALIVE law's ranges.
- Ice rink: three snow places would be one too many; its ice goes to the bath and the mountain.
